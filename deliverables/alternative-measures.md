# Alternative Measures Mapping

**Organisation:** CloudNative Analytics Ltd
**Prepared By:** Taimur Ijlal — GRC Analyst
**Date:** 2026-11-07

This document demonstrates that where controls have been excluded from the Statement of Applicability, the underlying risks are still addressed through alternative controls. ISO 27001:2022 permits exclusions provided the organisation can justify that the risk is either not present or is mitigated by other means. This mapping provides that justification.

---

## Physical Controls — Alternative Measures

The majority of exclusions (11 of 13) are physical controls that do not apply to a fully remote, cloud-native organisation. However, the risks these controls were designed to address — unauthorised access, environmental damage, equipment security — still exist in different forms and are addressed through logical and technical controls.

### Mapping Table

| **7.1** Physical security perimeters | Prevent unauthorised physical access to information processing facilities | 8.20 (Network security) — VPC isolation, security groups, NACLs create a "logical perimeter" around data processing resources. 8.1 (Endpoint devices) — encrypted endpoints are the physical boundary of company data. | AWS VPC architecture is a stronger perimeter than most physical buildings — security group rules are evaluated per-packet, and no tailgating is possible. Endpoint encryption means the physical device itself is the perimeter: even with physical access, data is inaccessible. | None identified. Logical perimeters provide equal or superior protection to physical perimeters for a cloud-native company. |

We can restrict access per resource, per time, per location (IP), with full audit trails. Badge cloning, tailgating, and social engineering attacks on reception staff are eliminated. | None. Logical access control provides superior granularity to physical entry control. |

## Summary: Risk Coverage Completeness

Every risk that the 13 excluded controls were designed to address is covered by one or more alternative controls. In several cases (notably 7.1, 7.2, 7.4), the alternative controls provide **superior protection** compared to the traditional physical controls because logical controls offer greater granularity, real-time monitoring, comprehensive audit trails, and are immune to physical attack vectors like tailgating and badge cloning.

The exclusions do not create security gaps — they reflect the reality that a cloud-native, fully remote organisation has a fundamentally different threat landscape from a traditional office-based company with on-premises infrastructure.
